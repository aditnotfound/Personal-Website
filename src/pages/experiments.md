# Experiments

half catalogue, half brag sheet. the "before" state is over; these actually exist.

## Veil

invisible desktop AI overlay. ask about what's on your screen, listen to system audio, keep notes in local RAG. bring your own keys. it's a Pluely fork with the license-server nonsense cut out, so the useful bits stay on your machine.

if you've seen Cluely, you know the genre: always there, barely visible, weirdly handy when it works.

## Orbit

a small app that makes your network do something besides rot in a spreadsheet. upload connections, poke a globe, get roasted for how useless the graph looks. Gemini under the hood.

tagline was "name your connections work for you." somehow that turned into a dashboard. i'm not complaining.

## PicoGPT

not mine originally (that's Kuber), but i keep mentioning it because it's dumb in the correct way: a whole GPT, train and inference, stuffed into a QR code. 39 lines of JS, about 4k params, runs in the browser when you scan it.

atomic as it gets. i like work that feels like a dare.
