<h1 class="research-title">Cognitive science, slowly.</h1>

<p class="research-lede">
  Adit Research Labs is a small, informal research program exploring how people
  form beliefs, update under uncertainty, and mistake confidence for understanding.
</p>

<p>
  We're interested in the boring-hard questions: attention, memory, judgment under
  noise, and why obvious experiments still feel surprising when you run them on
  yourself. Mostly behavioral. Occasionally computational. Always underpowered.
</p>

## Current focus

- belief updating when feedback is delayed or ambiguous
- metacognition in everyday decisions (not just psych undergrads)
- lightweight replication of classic paradigms with modern stimuli

## Coming soon

Pre-registered studies, open materials, and a proper lab note when there's
something worth citing instead of vibe-checking.
