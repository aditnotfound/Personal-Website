# Anthropic is a Cartel

a draft. real argument coming. for now, the title is the thesis.

## the setup

The big model providers don't behave like a market. They behave like a club. Coordinated pricing, coordinated talking points, and a synchronised emergency around "safety" that — funny enough — keeps every newcomer at a polite, expensive distance.

## the claim

Safety, as currently performed by the frontier labs, is doing the work that patents and licensing used to do for previous monopolies. It is a moat dressed up as a mission. And Anthropic is the cleanest case study of how that moat is built — not because they are the worst actor, but because they are the most articulate one.

## why it matters

Whoever defines the word "safe" in a year that matters gets to define who is allowed to build. That's not a research question. That's a regulatory one. And it should be argued in public, not adjudicated inside one company's policy team.
